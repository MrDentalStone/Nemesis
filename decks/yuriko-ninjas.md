## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 2 or 3 lands plus at least one 1 drop evasive creature (Ornithopter, Faerie Seer, Slither Blade, Changeling Outcast). Fast mana or a cantrip makes it a strong keep. Hands with no early attacker go back.
- Turn 1 or 2: play an evasive 1 drop and attack. Ninjutsu Yuriko from the command zone (cost U B) by returning the unblocked attacker; say "ninjutsu, returning Faerie Seer, Yuriko comes in tapped and attacking". Commander ninjutsu still adds commander tax, track it.
- Yuriko trigger: whenever a Ninja you control deals combat damage to a player, reveal the top card, put it in hand, and each opponent loses life equal to its mana value. Say the card and the number loud, then let each player move their own tracker.
- Stack the top before combat: Brainstorm, Ponder, Preordain, Sensei's Divining Top, Scroll Rack, Lim-Dûl's Vault, Mystical Tutor and Vampiric Tutor set up Draco (16), Commit // Memory (10, listed as Commit), Treasure Cruise and Dig Through Time (8), Gurmag Angler, Murktide Regent, Hullbreaker Horror and Silent-Blade Oni (7).
- Ninjas to swap in: Ingenious Infiltrator and Moon-Circuit Hacker draw, Fallen Shinobi and Silent-Blade Oni steal cards, Higure tutors another ninja, Thousand-Faced Shadow and Sakashima's Student copy, Satoru makes ninjutsu cheaper and reveals more cards. Re-use returned 1 drops every turn.
- Protection and interaction: Fierce Guardianship, Counterspell, Swan Song and Spell Pierce protect the key turn; Cyclonic Rift, Toxic Deluge, Snuff Out and Fatal Push clear blockers. Rhystic Study and Mystic Remora pay you for each spell.
- Full Send: count lethal out loud before combat (each Yuriko hit times 3 opponents, plus combat damage). Stack the top with your biggest mana value before the ninja connects.
