## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 lands, or 2 lands plus Sol Ring, Mana Vault, a Talisman or a mana dork, and at least one Treasure or Food maker. Dig with Demonic or Vampiric Tutor if the hand has lands but no plan.
- Turns 1 to 3: ramp and set up fodder (Gilded Goose, Tireless Provisioner, Trail of Crumbs, Witch's Oven, Pitiless Plunderer). Aim to cast Korvold on turn 3 or 4 with a Treasure or Food ready to sacrifice to its own enter trigger.
- Korvold trigger: whenever Korvold enters or attacks you sacrifice another permanent, then each time you sacrifice a permanent Korvold gets a +1/+1 counter and you draw 1. Say each trigger out loud and keep the counter on the card with mark. Sacrifice Treasures and Food first, lands only when it wins.
- Engines: Ashnod's Altar or Phyrexian Altar plus Pitiless Plunderer turn every creature death into mana and cards. Mayhem Devil and Goblin Bombardment punish each sacrifice. Skullclamp on 1 toughness tokens draws 2 per clamp.
- Combo line: Gravecrawler, Phyrexian Altar and Pitiless Plunderer loops forever (sac Gravecrawler for B, Plunderer makes a Treasure, recast from the yard). With Blood Artist, Zulaport Cutthroat, Mayhem Devil or Bastion of Remembrance on board that drains the table. Tutors find the missing piece. State the loop once, count the result, ask for responses, then resolve it.
- Interaction: hold Abrupt Decay, Assassin's Trophy or Infernal Grasp for the most dangerous permanent; Toxic Deluge or Blasphemous Act when behind on board. Keep Heroic Intervention or Greaves for Korvold.
- Full Send: count lethal out loud before swinging (Korvold power after its attack trigger, Revel in Riches at 10 Treasures on upkeep, drain triggers). Target the player most likely to win next, not the weakest.
- Life from Korvold draws is free, but Mana Vault and Vampiric Tutor cost life; adjust_life yourself for each.
