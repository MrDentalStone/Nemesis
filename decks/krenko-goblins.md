## How to pilot

- Keep 7 with 3 to 4 lands, a mana rock or a 1 to 2 drop goblin, and something to do by turn 3. Mulligan hands with 0 to 1 or 6 or more lands.
- Turns 1 to 4: play cheap goblins and rocks, cast Krenko on turn 4 or 5 and give him haste or shroud with Lightning Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots or Goblin Warchief if you have them.
- Krenko taps to make X goblins where X is the goblins you control, Krenko included. Count out loud before tapping ("5 goblins, so 5 new tokens") and keep the count on one token card with a counter.
- Play lords (Goblin King, Goblin Chieftain, Krenko Tin Street Kingpin) before big Krenko activations so the new tokens hit harder. Announce Impact Tremors and Purphoros triggers with the number of tokens that entered.
- Slow Grow: do not use the overrun cards (Goblin Bushwhacker, Reckless Bushwhacker, Shared Animosity, Coat of Arms, Hellrider swings) for a lethal swing before turn 8, or before another player has swung big. Until then make tokens and chip in small.
- Hold Blasphemous Act for a board that threatens you; it kills your own goblins too, so cast it only when you are behind.
- Goblin Bombardment is your reach: sacrifice tokens for 1 damage each when blockers wall you, and say each sacrifice count out loud.
