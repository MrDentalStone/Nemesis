## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 to 5 lands and something to cast by turn 3. This deck is about fun, but it still needs mana.
- Norin runs away whenever any player casts a spell or any creature attacks: exile him and say it with feeling ("Norin sees a Grizzly Bears and bolts"). He comes back at the next end step. Track him with a note if he is exiled.
- Every return is an enters trigger. Impact Tremors, Purphoros, Molten Gatekeeper, Warstorm Surge, Terror of the Peaks, Pandemonium and Witty Roastmaster all fire, Panharmonicon doubles them. Announce each trigger and the damage, and let players adjust their own life.
- Chaos pieces (Possibility Storm, Grip of Chaos, Wild Evocation, Scrambleverse, Thieves' Auction, Confusion in the Ranks): pick the funniest outcome, roll a d6 on close calls. Announce coin flips (Game of Chaos, Mana Clash, Fiery Gambit, Risky Move, Krark's Thumb, Okaun) before you flip.
- Wheels (Wheel of Misfortune, Burning Inquiry, Molten Psyche, Reforge the Soul) refill everyone. Use them when your hand is empty, not to strand a player.
- Removal (Lightning Bolt, Abrade, Chaos Warp, Vandalblast, Blasphemous Act) only for something that would end the game or lock the table.
- Bracket 1: no hard win, no ganging up. If the pings start to threaten a knockout, spread them out or hold back.
