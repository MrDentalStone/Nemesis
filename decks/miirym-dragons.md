## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 to 5 lands plus a ramp piece (Sol Ring, Signet, a mana elf or a 2 drop land spell). A hand of only dragons and 2 lands goes back.
- Turns 1 to 4: ramp first. Land drop every turn, cast Cultivate, Kodama's Reach, Three Visits, Dragonspeaker Shaman or Dragon's Hoard before anything else. Aim to cast Miirym on turn 4 or 5 with Greaves or Boots ready if possible.
- Miirym trigger: whenever another nontoken Dragon enters, you make a token copy that is not legendary. Say it out loud every time ("Miirym trigger, copying Thundermaw"), then spawn one token and mark its P/T and haste.
- Best payoffs with Miirym out: Terror of the Peaks (each copy and each dragon pings), Thundermaw Hellkite (taps fliers twice), Lathliss, Utvara Hellkite and Dragon Broodmother make extra dragons. Dragon Tempest gives haste and damage per dragon entering, so the copy counts too.
- Card flow comes from Harmonize, Garruk's Uprising, Return of the Wildspeaker, Temur Ascendancy, Elemental Bond and Kindred Discovery. Keep Counterspell or Swan Song up for a board wipe aimed at your dragons once you have 3 or more.
- Interaction: Beast Within and Chaos Warp for the scariest permanent, Nature's Claim for artifacts and enchantments, Heroic Intervention when a wipe is cast.
- Slow Grow: build the dragon board, hold Balefire Dragon, Scourge of Valkas pings and Atarka until the table is big; attack the player who is ahead, not the weakest.
- Full Send: count lethal out loud before swinging (fliers, haste from Tempest or Hellkite Charger, Terror pings). Hellkite Charger with spare mana gives extra combats; Kessig Wolf Run and Dragonlord Atarka push the last points through.
