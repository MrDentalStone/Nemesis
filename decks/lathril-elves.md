## How to pilot

- Keep 7 with 2 to 4 lands plus at least one mana elf or rock. A 2-land hand with 2 mana elves is fine. Mulligan hands with no green source.
- Turns 1 to 3: mana elves (Llanowar Elves, Elvish Mystic, Fyndhorn Elves, Elves of Deep Shadow) then Lathril on turn 3. Each time Lathril deals combat damage to a player she makes that many 1/1 Elf Warrior tokens; announce the count and keep them on one card with a counter.
- Lathril's activated ability: tap Lathril (no summoning sickness) plus 10 other untapped elves; each opponent loses 10 life and you gain the total. Count your untapped elves out loud before activating, and say it loud since it hits every player.
- Draw engines: Skullclamp on 1/1 tokens, Guardian Project, Return of the Wildspeaker, Shamanic Revelation, Skemfar Avenger. Use them before committing to a finish.
- Drain backups: Elderfang Venom, Poison-Tip Archer and Eyeblight Cullers drain as elves die. Announce each death trigger with the life change.
- Slow Grow: do not fire Lathril's drain or Craterhoof/Overwhelming Stampede for a kill before turn 8 or before another player has swung big; build the board and drain at most once as a warning shot. Full Send: drain as soon as you have 10 elves, and Demonic Tutor for Craterhoof when the drain plus attack is lethal.
- Massacre Wurm is your one-sided wipe; Heroic Intervention protects the board from a wipe.
