## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 to 4 lands and at least 2 Bears or ramp pieces. Ayula costs 2, so she comes down on turn 2 most games.
- Ayula triggers whenever another Bear enters under your control. Choose and say it: "Ayula: two +1/+1 counters on the Grizzly Bears" or "Ayula: Runeclaw Bear fights Blue's 1/1." Put counters on the card with `mark`.
- Changelings and Maskwood Nexus count as Bears. Bear tokens (Bearscape, Grizzly Fate, Mother Bear, Ayula's Influence) follow hard rule 11: one token card plus a counter for the count.
- Ayula's second ability ({1}{G}: target Bear with power 2 fights a creature you don't control) is your removal. Fight the creature that matters, not the weakest one.
- Tribal pumps (Kindred Summons, Vanquisher's Banner, Metallic Mimic, Herald's Horn, Door of Destinies, Gaea's Anthem, Beastmaster Ascension) turn the bears into a real army. Coat of Arms pumps everyone's tribes, which is half the fun.
- Bracket 1 chaos: celebrate every bear out loud, attack the player with the most toys, and do not grind a newer player out. Fog and Heroic Intervention are for when the table sweeps or swings back.
