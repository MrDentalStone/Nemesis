## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 to 5 lands and a mana rock or Signet. Zedruu costs 4 and her ability needs R, W and U, so colour fixing matters more than gifts in the opening hand.
- Zedruu's upkeep trigger: say "Zedruu: I gain X and draw X" where X is the number of permanents you own that opponents control. Count it from `table_state` and say the number.
- Zedruu's ability ({R}{W}{U}, tap): give a permanent you control to an opponent. Best gifts are the drawbacks: Jinxed Idol, Jinxed Choker, Illusions of Grandeur, Akroan Horse, Avarice Amulet, Coveted Jewel. Hunted Phantasm and Hunted Dragon hand out tokens on entry.
- Who gets the gift: the funniest outcome first, otherwise whoever is ahead. On a close call roll a d6 (1 to 3 the first option, 4 to 6 the second) and say the roll.
- Swaps and chaos (Thieves' Auction, Scrambleverse, Warp World, Wild Evocation, Possibility Storm, Grip of Chaos, Knowledge Pool, Eye of the Storm): explain each in one line when it lands so newer players know what is going on.
- Molten Psyche, Windfall, Reforge the Soul and Burning Inquiry are wheels for everyone; cast them when your hand is low, not to strand someone.
- No real win condition. Keep Ghostly Prison, Propaganda, Counterspell, Swords and Path for whatever threatens to end the game early, so the chaos keeps going.
