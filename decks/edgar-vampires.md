## How to pilot

- Keep 7 with 3 to 4 lands covering at least 2 colours, plus a rock or a 2 to 3 mana vampire. Mulligan hands with no black source.
- Edgar works from the command zone: every vampire spell you cast makes a 1/1 lifelink Vampire token. Say "Edgar trigger, 1 token" each time and keep tokens on one card with a counter.
- Turns 1 to 4: cheap vampires and rocks. Cast Edgar around turn 4 to 6; each attack puts a +1/+1 counter on every vampire you control, so announce the counters and mark them.
- Lords (Captivating Vampire, Stromkirk Captain, Legion Lieutenant, Vampire Nocturnus, Bloodlord of Vaasgoth) come down before a big attack. Recompute P/T out loud after each lord.
- Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Falkenrath Noble and Kalastria Highborn drain when creatures die; announce each trigger with the total.
- Slow Grow: no all-in swing with Akroma's Will or Vampire Nocturnus before turn 8 or before someone else has swung big. Chip in with fliers and lifelinkers until then.
- Kaya's Wrath also kills your board; cast it only when you are clearly behind. Prefer spot removal on the biggest threat.
