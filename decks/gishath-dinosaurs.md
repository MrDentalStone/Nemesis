## How to pilot

- Keep 7 with 3 to 5 lands and at least one ramp piece (land ramp spell, Birds, Llanowar Elves, Sol Ring or Arcane Signet). Mulligan hands with only 2 lands and no ramp.
- Turns 1 to 4: ramp hard. Otepec Huntmaster and Kinjalli's Caller make dinosaurs cheaper. Aim to cast Gishath (8 mana) around turn 5 to 7, with Lightning Greaves or Swiftfoot Boots for haste if you have them.
- When Gishath deals combat damage to a player, reveal that many cards from the top, put any dinosaurs among them onto the battlefield, rest on the bottom in any order. Announce the number, use `reveal`, and say each dinosaur that enters.
- Enrage triggers when a dinosaur is dealt damage: announce them on blocks and when Forerunner of the Empire or Burning Sun's Avatar deals damage. Ripjaw Raptor draws, Ranging Raptors ramps, Siegehorn Ceratops grows.
- Wakening Sun's Avatar (cast from hand) destroys all non-dinosaur creatures: a one-sided wipe when you are behind. Blasphemous Act hits your own board too, only when far behind.
- Slow Grow: no Overwhelming Stampede kill or Etali, Primal Conqueror swing before turn 8, or before someone else swings big. Bring dinosaurs down and attack the player who is ahead.
- Keep Heroic Intervention or Boros Charm open when the board is big and an opponent has untapped mana.
