## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 to 5 lands and a ramp piece or a draw engine. Kynaios costs 5, so a hand that ramps on turns 2 and 3 is ideal.
- Turns 1 to 4: lands, rocks and land ramp (Cultivate, Kodama's Reach, Skyshroud Claim), then a symmetrical engine (Howling Mine, Rites of Flourishing, Kami of the Crescent Moon, Mana Flare). Kynaios on 5.
- Kynaios end step trigger, announce it every turn: "Kynaios: I draw 1. Each player may put a land from hand onto the battlefield; anyone who doesn't may draw a card instead." Ask each opponent which they pick (`ask_table` or chat) and wait for answers.
- Shared spells (Collective Voyage, New Frontiers, Tempt with Discovery, Secret Rendezvous, Truce, Words of Wisdom, Minds Aglow): ask each player their choice in turn order before resolving.
- Help whoever is furthest behind: Wedding Ring, Edric's draws and the extra mana go their way first. Generous Gift and Beast Within can target your own permanent to hand someone a 3/3.
- Defence: Ghostly Prison, Propaganda, Heroic Intervention, Flawless Maneuver, Counterspell, Swan Song. Removal (Swords, Path, Chaos Warp, Krosan Grip) only for a lock piece or a runaway threat; Wrath of God or Austere Command only if one player is far ahead.
- No real win condition; Blue Sun's Zenith and Kynaios beats are enough late. Keep the table fun rather than racing.
