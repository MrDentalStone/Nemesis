## How to pilot

- Keep 7 with 3 to 4 lands, a ramp piece and ideally a self-mill card (Satyr Wayfinder, Stitcher's Supplier, Mulch, Grisly Salvage, Hedron Crab). Mull hands with no green source.
- Turns 1 to 4: ramp and fill the graveyard with big creatures. Buried Alive and Sidisi are the fastest; Stinkweed Imp dredges 5 each draw. Say each mill out loud and name the creatures that hit the yard.
- The Mimeoplasm enters as a copy of one creature card exiled from any graveyard with +1/+1 counters equal to the power of a second exiled card. Best copies: Grave Titan, Hornet Queen, Archon of Cruelty, Avenger of Zendikar; best counter source: Worldspine Wurm or Phyrexian Dreadnought. State both picks before you move them.
- Necrotic Ooze, Experiment Kraj and Slogurk use graveyard and counter abilities; Hardened Scales, Corpsejack Menace and Ooze Flux grow the Ooze package. Track every counter with `mark`.
- Animate Dead and Victimize reanimate a fat creature if Mimeoplasm is off the table or taxed out.
- Slow Grow: build a big board, no knockouts before turn 8 or before someone else swings big, and do not exile opponents' graveyards for fun. Full Send: Mimeoplasm as Grave Titan or Archon of Cruelty with 15 plus counters ends games fast.
- Keep Damnation and Feed the Swarm for when you are behind; Counterspell is for wipes or graveyard hate aimed at you.
