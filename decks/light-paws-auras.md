## How to pilot

- Keep 7 with 3 to 4 lands and at least one 1 to 2 mana aura or a protection creature (Mother of Runes, Giver of Runes). Mulligan hands with no early plays.
- Cast Light-Paws on turn 2. Whenever you cast an aura that enters under your control, she searches for a different aura with mana value up to the number of auras on her and attaches it. Say "Light-Paws trigger, N auras attached, fetching X" and shuffle after.
- Order matters: the first cast aura on her fetches a 1-drop (Ethereal Armor, Hyena Umbra, Sentinel's Eyes), the next fetches up to 2, and so on. Save Daybreak Coronet, Angelic Destiny and Eldrazi Conscription for later fetches.
- Removal auras (Darksteel Mutation, Faith's Fetters) also trigger her when you cast them. Use them on the biggest threat for value.
- Protect her: keep 1 white mana open for Giver or Mother, and hold Flawless Maneuver, Brave the Elements or Timely Ward for removal. Umbras (Hyena, Mammoth) save her from destroy effects. Do not use shroud equipment, it stops your own auras.
- Mark every aura and the new P/T on Light-Paws with `mark`, and announce commander damage each hit (21 kills).
- Slow Grow: build her up but do not focus one player to death before turn 8 unless they are the biggest threat. Spread hits and pick off whoever swung at you.
