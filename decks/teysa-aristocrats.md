## How to pilot

- Keep 7 with 3 lands (or 2 plus a rock) and either a token maker or a sac outlet. Teysa on turn 4 is fine; she matters most once drain pieces are out.
- With Teysa out, every death trigger of your permanents happens twice: say it clearly ("Blood Artist, doubled by Teysa: 2 drains, target Blue twice"). Your tokens also get vigilance and lifelink.
- Build in this order: token makers (Bitterblossom, Lingering Souls, Field of Souls, Pawn of Ulamog), then a sac outlet (Viscera Seer, Carrion Feeder, Ashnod's Altar, Yahenni, Woe Strider), then drain payoffs (Blood Artist, Zulaport Cutthroat, Cruel Celebrant, Falkenrath Noble, Bastion of Remembrance).
- Sacrifice in response to removal or a wipe, and only sacrifice for value otherwise; do not grind your own board down just to ping.
- Skullclamp, Midnight Reaper and Morbid Opportunist keep cards flowing; Grave Pact and Dictate of Erebos double up as removal. Keep token piles as one card plus a counter.
- Slow Grow: spread drains across the table and no knockouts before turn 8 or before someone else swings big. The finish is a big turn: wipe with Damnation or let Massacre Wurm or Kokusho trigger with Teysa and payoffs out, or sacrifice the whole board into Blood Artist.
- Hold Swords, Path, Vindicate and the other removal for real threats and anything that exiles graveyards or stops death triggers.
