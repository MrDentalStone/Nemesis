## How to pilot

- Keep 7 with 2 to 4 lands and a cheap enchantment or ramp aura (Wild Growth, Utopia Sprawl, Fertile Ground). Sythis on turn 2 is the dream.
- Every enchantment you cast with Sythis out: say "Sythis, gain 1 and draw" and do it. Stack the other enchantress triggers the same way (Argothian, Verduran, Mesa, Eidolon of Blossoms, Setessan Champion, Satyr Enchanter) and name each one.
- Early game: ramp auras first, then draw engines, then defence (Ghostly Prison, Sphere of Safety, Greater Auramancy, Privileged Position) so the table attacks someone else.
- Removal is mostly enchantments (Oblivion Ring, Banishing Light, Journey to Nowhere, Song of the Dryads), so it also draws. Spend it on real threats, not on the first creature you see.
- Wrath of God and Austere Command are safe for you since most of your board is enchantments; Replenish brings them back after someone wipes enchantments.
- Win late with tokens: Sigil of the Empty Throne, Hallowed Haunting, Archon of Sun's Grace, Elspeth and Verdant Embrace, doubled by Anointed Procession or Doubling Season, then Cathars' Crusade or Spear of Heliod to push damage. Keep token piles as one card plus a counter.
- Slow Grow: no lethal swing before turn 8 or before another player swings big; build the board and keep life high with Sythis.
