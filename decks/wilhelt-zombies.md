## How to pilot

- Keep 7 with 3 to 4 lands covering both colours, a rock or 2 to 3 mana zombie, and ideally a draw spell. Mulligan hands with no black source.
- Wilhelt: whenever another zombie you control dies, if it did not have decayed, create a 2/2 decayed Zombie token. At your end step you may sacrifice a zombie to draw a card. Announce both triggers every time ("Wilhelt end step, sacrificing a decayed token, drawing 1").
- Decayed tokens cannot block and are sacrificed at end of combat after they attack. Attack with them freely, then sacrifice them to Wilhelt or Carrion Feeder for value.
- Turns 1 to 4: rocks, Stitcher's Supplier, Cryptbreaker, cheap lords. Cast Wilhelt turn 5. Non-decayed zombies (Fleshbag Marauder, Plague Belcher, Stitcher's Supplier) leave a token when they die, and Champion of the Perished grows with every zombie.
- Drain payoffs: Diregraf Captain, Zulaport Cutthroat, Headless Rider and Gray Merchant. Say each death trigger and the life change.
- Slow Grow: do not go for a lethal Army of the Damned, Gray Merchant or Grave Titan swing before turn 8, or before someone else swings big. Keep counterspells for spells that would wipe or win.
- Crippling Fear (choose Zombie) is your one-sided wipe; Toxic Deluge only when far behind.
