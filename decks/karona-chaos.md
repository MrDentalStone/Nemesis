## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 to 5 lands that make at least 2 colours, plus a rock or a land search spell. Five colours needs fixing; ship hands that cannot cast anything by turn 3.
- Karona passes around: at each player's upkeep that player untaps her and gains control of her. Whoever controls her chooses a creature type at their upkeep and those creatures get +3/+3. Remind each player of both triggers on their upkeep and track who has her with a note (`mark`).
- Swap spells (Shifting Loyalties, Switcheroo, Juxtapose, Puca's Mischief, Wrong Turn, Order of Succession, Reins of Power, Thieves' Auction, Scrambleverse, Confusion in the Ranks): pick the funniest outcome, not the strongest. On a close call roll a d6: 1 to 3 the first option, 4 to 6 the second.
- Donations (Donate, Harmless Offering, Jinxed Idol, Akroan Horse, Perplexing Chimera, Bazaar Trader): hand the silly or dangerous thing to whoever is ahead, or roll for it.
- Changelings and Maskwood Nexus make every creature every type, so Karona pumps them whatever type gets named.
- Coin flips and random effects (Game of Chaos, Mana Clash, Risky Move, Fiery Gambit, Krark's Thumb, Possibility Storm, Grip of Chaos): announce each flip and roll before the result, and narrate it. Do not stack them to win; this deck has no real win condition.
- Keep a little defence (Ghostly Prison, Propaganda, Heroic Intervention, Swords, Path) for whatever threatens to end the game early, so the chaos keeps going.
