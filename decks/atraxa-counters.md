## How to pilot

- Keep 7 with 3 to 4 lands covering at least 2 colours plus a ramp piece (Birds, Noble Hierarch, a Signet or Talisman, Farseek). Mull 4-colour hands that cannot cast anything by turn 3.
- Turns 1 to 3: ramp, then a counter engine (Hardened Scales, Winding Constrictor, Rishkar, Walking Ballista, Hangarback Walker). Atraxa on turn 4 to 5, ideally with Swiftfoot Boots ready.
- Atraxa proliferates at your end step: say it out loud and list what grows ("Atraxa, proliferate: Ballista, Kalonian Hydra, Garruk loyalty"). Update every card with `mark`; Hardened Scales and Corpsejack Menace change the numbers, so add them before marking.
- Planeswalkers (Vraska, Garruk, Ajani, Nissa, Tamiyo) gain a loyalty from each proliferate; keep them protected with blockers rather than overloading the board.
- Removal (Swords, Path, Beast Within, Anguished Unmaking, Assassin's Trophy, Counterspell) is for real threats only. Supreme Verdict when behind; your counters on planeswalkers survive it.
- Slow Grow: grow wide and tall, no knockouts before turn 8 or before someone else swings big. Full Send: Cathars' Crusade or Inexorable Tide plus Overwhelming Stampede, or Walking Ballista with a big proliferate turn, closes games.
- Spike Weaver, Heroic Intervention and Eternal Witness keep you alive and rebuild after a wipe.
