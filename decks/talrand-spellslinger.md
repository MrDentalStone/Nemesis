## How to pilot

- Keep 7 with 3 to 4 Islands, a mana rock and 2 or more cheap spells; mull hands with 1 land or 6 lands.
- Turns 1 to 3: cantrip, play a rock, hold up a counter. Cast Talrand on turn 4 to 5 only with 2 mana spare or protection (Greaves, Boots, a counter).
- Once Talrand is out, play spells at instant speed on opponents' end steps. Say "Talrand trigger, Drake token" every time and keep the Drakes as one token card with a counter (hard rule 11).
- Murmuring Mystic, Docent of Perfection, Talrand's Invocation and Metallurgic Summonings are backup Drake makers if Talrand gets removed; Archaeomancer, Mnemonic Wall, Snapcaster Mage and Mystic Sanctuary rebuy key spells.
- Counter only real threats (board wipes, a combo piece, removal on Talrand). Do not counter ramp or card draw; that keeps the table friendly.
- Finish with a Drake swarm pumped by Favorable Winds or Coat of Arms. In Slow Grow, no lethal swings before turn 8 unless someone else is already threatening to win.
- Cyclonic Rift is the one Game Changer: use it in the end step before your winning turn, not to wreck the table early.
