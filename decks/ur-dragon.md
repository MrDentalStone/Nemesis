## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 to 5 lands that make at least 3 colours plus a ramp spell. Two-land hands with only dragons go back; colour fixing matters more than speed.
- Eminence works from the command zone: your Dragon spells cost 1 less even before Ur-Dragon is cast. Remind the table once per game.
- Turns 1 to 4: ramp (Cultivate, Kodama's Reach, Three Visits, Farseek, Signet, Chromatic Lantern). Cheap dragons like Dragonspeaker Shaman and Scion of the Ur-Dragon come next, then a big dragon on turn 4 or 5.
- Ur-Dragon trigger: whenever one or more Dragons you control attack, draw that many cards, then you may put a permanent card from your hand onto the battlefield. Say it loud with the count ("3 dragons attacking, draw 3, then I put in Terror of the Peaks").
- Best drops off that trigger: Terror of the Peaks, Dragon Tempest, Utvara Hellkite, Lathliss or Dragon Broodmother. Haste comes from Dragon Tempest, Greaves, Boots, Thundermaw and Glorybringer.
- Interaction: Swords to Plowshares, Anguished Unmaking, Beast Within, Chaos Warp for threats; Counterspell or Swan Song for a wipe; Crux of Fate (non-Dragon mode) clears the board but keeps yours. Heroic Intervention or Boros Charm when a wipe resolves.
- Slow Grow: build up, attack with 1 or 2 dragons at the player who is ahead, and hold Balefire Dragon, Scourge of Valkas and Crux of Fate until the table has gone big.
- Full Send: count lethal out loud before swinging, including Terror pings, Dragon Tempest damage, Kolaghan, the Storm's Fury pump and Dragonlord Kolaghan haste. Dragonlord Atarka and Scourge of Valkas clear blockers first.
