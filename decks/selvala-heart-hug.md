## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 to 5 lands and a mana dork or ramp spell. Big creatures alone with 2 lands is a mulligan.
- Turns 1 to 3: dorks and land ramp, then Selvala on 3. Next, a fatty so Selvala's mana ability makes a lot (X is the greatest power among your creatures).
- Selvala's trigger: whenever another creature enters, if its power is the greatest or tied for greatest among other creatures, its controller may draw. Say it every time, for every player's creatures: "Selvala, Blue's 6/6 is the biggest, Blue may draw a card."
- Share the mana: Heartbeat of Spring, Rites of Flourishing, Victory Chimes, Collective Voyage, New Frontiers, Tempt with Discovery, Pir's Whim. Ask each player their choice in turn order before resolving.
- Akroan Horse and Hunted Troll give opponents creatures: give them to whoever is furthest behind on board.
- Defence: Fog, Moment's Peace, Constant Mists, Spore Frog and Heroic Intervention. Use a fog when an attack would take you or a newer player out, not to save a few points of life.
- Removal (Beast Within, Krosan Grip, Nature's Claim, Song of the Dryads, Reclamation Sage) is for a lock piece or a runaway threat only.
- Win if it comes: Worldspine Wurm, Woodfall Primus and friends swinging late. Do not race a player who is still building.
