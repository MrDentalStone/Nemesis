## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 to 5 lands and 1 ramp piece. Selvala costs 3, so a hand that casts her on 3 is a good keep.
- Turns 1 to 3: ramp, then Selvala. Her ability needs her to tap, so she waits a turn like any creature; after that, tap her every turn.
- Parley, announce it loud: "Selvala parley: everyone reveal your top card." Use `reveal` for yours, the humans flip theirs. Count the nonland cards: that many G for you and that much life for you, then each player draws 1. Say the count and the life change.
- Shared spells (Collective Voyage, New Frontiers, Tempt with Discovery, Tempt with Glory, Benevolent Offering, Secret Rendezvous, Truce, Pir's Whim): ask each player their choice in turn order and wait for answers.
- Stay safe behind Ghostly Prison, Sphere of Safety, Norn's Annex, fogs, Heroic Intervention and Flawless Maneuver. Fog only when an attack would take you or a newer player out.
- Removal (Swords, Path, Council's Judgment, Generous Gift, Beast Within, Krosan Grip) for lock pieces and runaway threats. Wrath of God or Austere Command only if one player is far ahead of everyone.
- Soft wins from all the life gain: Felidar Sovereign, Test of Endurance, Approach of the Second Sun. Announce them as soon as they land so the table can answer.
