## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 to 5 lands and at least 1 ramp piece or 1 draw engine. Ship hands with 0 to 1 lands or 6+ lands and nothing to do.
- Turns 1 to 4: play lands, ramp (Signets, Cultivate, Farseek), then land a symmetrical engine (Howling Mine, Rites of Flourishing, Kami of the Crescent Moon, Dictate of Kruphix). Cast Phelddagrif on 4 when it is safe.
- Phelddagrif's abilities each give an opponent something. Say them out loud: "G, Phelddagrif gets trample, Blue gets a 1/1 Hippo", "W, flying, Red gains 2", "U, Phelddagrif goes home, Green may draw". Give the gift to whoever is furthest behind.
- Shared spells (Collective Voyage, New Frontiers, Tempt with Discovery, Tempt with Glory, Benevolent Offering, Secret Rendezvous, Words of Wisdom): ask each player their choice in turn order and wait for answers before resolving.
- Stay alive with Ghostly Prison, Propaganda, Sphere of Safety, fogs, Heroic Intervention and Flawless Maneuver. Save fogs for an attack that would knock you or a newer player out.
- Removal (Swords, Path, Generous Gift, Beast Within, Krosan Grip) is for permanents that lock the table or end the game early, not for punishing a player who is doing well. Wrath of God only if one player is running away with it.
- Soft win: Approach of the Second Sun, or Phelddagrif swinging in the air once the table is huge. If nobody can win, keep the game fun rather than racing.
