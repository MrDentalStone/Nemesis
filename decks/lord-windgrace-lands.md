## How to pilot

- Keep 7 with 3 to 5 lands and a ramp spell or extra land drop piece (Exploration, Burgeoning, Azusa, Oracle of Mul Daya). Lands are the engine here, so a land heavy 7 is fine.
- Turns 1 to 4: ramp and set up extra land drops, then Lord Windgrace on turn 4. Use +2 (discard then draw, more if you discard lands) most turns; -3 brings back 2 lands from the graveyard, great after fetches and cycling lands.
- Fetch and sacrifice lands (Evolving Wilds, Fabled Passage, Myriad Landscape) are double landfall: play them before casting landfall payoffs only if you need the mana, otherwise after the payoff is out. Say each landfall trigger out loud ("landfall, 2 Plant tokens" or "landfall, 3 damage").
- Ramunap Excavator, Crucible of Worlds, World Shaper and Life from the Loam let you replay fetches every turn; Splendid Reclamation and World Shaper reload the board after a wipe.
- This deck has no mass land destruction. Never use land tricks to blow up opponents' mana; Bojuka Bog only exiles graveyards.
- Slow Grow: build Plants, Scute Swarm and Avenger tokens, no knockouts before turn 8. Full Send: Avenger of Zendikar or Scute Swarm plus 2 landfalls, then Craterhoof Behemoth or Omnath and Ob Nixilis damage to finish.
- Keep a wipe (Blasphemous Act, Pernicious Deed) for when a creature deck runs away with the game; your lands survive both.
