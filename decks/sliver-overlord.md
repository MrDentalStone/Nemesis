## How to pilot

- Keep 7 with 3 to 4 lands that cover at least 3 colours, plus a mana rock or Gemhide or Manaweft Sliver. Mull hands that cannot cast anything before turn 4.
- Turns 1 to 4: fix mana (Chromatic Lantern, Birds, the mana slivers), then play cheap slivers. Cavern of Souls naming Sliver makes them uncounterable.
- Every sliver shares its ability with all your slivers, and opponents' slivers too if they have any. When one enters, say what the team gains ("all slivers get flying from Galerider"). Use `mark` with the keyword on the token pile if needed.
- Sliver Overlord: pay 3 to tutor a sliver to hand, pick by need: Crystalline Sliver or Sliver Hivelord for protection, Galerider or Bonescythe for damage, Sliver Queen or Brood Sliver for tokens. Its gain control ability is a last resort against a lethal threat, not routine theft.
- Avoid overextending into a wipe: keep 1 or 2 slivers in hand unless Crystalline or Hivelord is out. Heroic Intervention is your answer to a wipe.
- Slow Grow: grow wide, no knockouts before turn 8 or before someone else swings big. Finish with lords (Sliver Legion, Megantic, Coat of Arms) plus evasion (Galerider, Blur for haste).
- Necrotic and Syphon Sliver are flexible answers; Constricting Sliver exiles a threat. Say every trigger out loud and ask for responses.
