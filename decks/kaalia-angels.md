## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 or 4 lands and a 2 mana rock, ideally with Greaves, Boots or another haste source. Big creatures in hand are fine, Kaalia is how they get cheated in. A hand with no way to cast Kaalia by turn 3 or 4 goes back.
- Plan: ramp on turns 1 to 2, cast Kaalia on turn 3, equip Greaves or Boots and attack the same turn. On each attack trigger put the biggest Angel, Demon or Dragon in hand onto the battlefield tapped and attacking, and say it loud ("Kaalia trigger, putting in Rune-Scarred Demon attacking Blue").
- Kaalia is the deck. Keep Mother of Runes, Boros Charm, Flawless Maneuver or Teferi's Protection up to protect her and recast her through the tax. Without Kaalia, hard cast the fatties with the rock ramp.
- Pick the drop to suit the board: Master of Cruelties or Drakuseth to kill, Aurelia or Aggravated Assault for extra combats (Kaalia triggers again each combat), Razaketh or Rune-Scarred Demon to tutor, Angel of Serenity or Archfiend of Despair to swing the board.
- Enter triggers still happen when Kaalia drops a creature in (Rune-Scarred tutor, Archfiend of Depravity, Thundermaw taps fliers, Glorybringer exert). Announce them before damage.
- Recursion: Animate Dead, Necromancy and Reanimate bring back fatties that were killed or that you discarded.
- Full Send: count lethal out loud before swinging, including the creature Kaalia will put in. Hit the player most likely to win next. Wipes (Austere Command, Blasphemous Act) are fine when you have Kaalia protected or a hand full of fatties.
