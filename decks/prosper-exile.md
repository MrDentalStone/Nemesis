## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 or 4 lands plus a 2 mana rock or an impulse draw spell. Prosper on turn 3 is the goal; hands with 2 lands and no rock go back.
- Prosper triggers: at your end step exile the top card, you may play it until your next end step. Whenever you play a card from exile, make a Treasure. Say both out loud ("Prosper end step, exiling Mayhem Devil" and "cast from exile, Treasure").
- Every exile source feeds Prosper: Light Up the Stage, Reckless Impulse, Wrenn's Resolve, Jeska's Will, Outpost Siege, Valakut Exploration, Laelia, Professional Face-Breaker, Chandra, Dauthi Voidwalker's void cards, Ragavan and Laughing Jasper Flint. Play exiled cards before cards in hand so nothing expires.
- Treasures are ammo: Disciple of the Vault, Marionette Master and Mayhem Devil turn each Treasure sacrificed into damage or life loss; Storm-Kiln Artist and Pitiless Plunderer make more; Kalain grows creatures; Revel in Riches wins at 10 Treasures on your upkeep. Keep the Treasure count as one token plus a counter.
- Drain board: Blood Artist, Zulaport Cutthroat, Bastion of Remembrance and Mirkwood Bats, with Goblin Bombardment or Viscera Seer as outlets. Announce each drain trigger with the totals.
- Interaction: Terminate, Infernal Grasp, Go for the Throat for creatures, Bedevil and Kolaghan's Command for artifacts, Chaos Warp for anything, Blasphemous Act or Toxic Deluge when behind.
- Slow Grow: build Treasures and card advantage, keep drains pointed at the leader, and hold Marionette Master and Revel in Riches until the table is big.
- Full Send: count lethal out loud before you start sacrificing (Treasures times Disciple, Mayhem Devil and Marionette damage plus drains), then do it in one go.
