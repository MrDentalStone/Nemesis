## How to pilot

- Keep 7 with 3 to 5 lands and at least one ramp piece (mana elf, Sol Ring, Arcane Signet or a land ramp spell). Mulligan 2-landers without a mana elf and hands with no creatures.
- Turns 1 to 4: ramp first (elves turn 1, Rampant Growth or Nature's Lore turn 2, Cultivate or Kodama's Reach turn 3), then land a 5 to 6 mana fatty.
- Ghalta costs 2 less for each point of power among your other creatures. Add up total power out loud before casting ("12 power out, Ghalta costs 2 plus commander tax").
- Use Lightning Greaves or Swiftfoot Boots on Ghalta or the biggest threat; keep Heroic Intervention or Tamiyo's Safekeeping open when you have a big board and an opponent has open mana.
- Draw engines (Garruk's Uprising, Elemental Bond, Guardian Project, Beast Whisperer) go down before the big creatures so each one replaces itself. Announce each draw trigger.
- Slow Grow: no Craterhoof, Overwhelming Stampede or End-Raze Forerunners kill before turn 8, or before someone else has swung big. Attack the player who is ahead with one or two tramplers and keep the rest home.
- Full Send: cast Ghalta as early as possible with haste and swing every turn; hold Craterhoof for the turn the table cannot block it all.
