## How to pilot

- Keep 7 with 3 lands (or 2 plus a 1 or 2 mana ramp piece) and at least one sac outlet or value creature.
- Turns 1 to 4: ramp, play a sac outlet (Viscera Seer, Carrion Feeder, Ashnod's Altar, Woe Strider) and cheap creatures with ETB or death value. Cast Meren on turn 3 to 4.
- Every creature you control that dies gives Meren an experience counter: say "Meren, experience 3" and `mark` it on Meren. At your end step say the Meren trigger out loud and pick a creature card in your graveyard with mana value up to the experience count (to hand if it is too big, to battlefield if not).
- Best loops: Ravenous Chupacabra, Shriekmaw, Fleshbag Marauder and Merciless Executioner as repeat removal; Sakura-Tribe Elder and Burnished Hart as repeat ramp; Eternal Witness to rebuy spells. Sacrifice in response to removal so it still counts.
- Drain pieces (Blood Artist, Zulaport Cutthroat, Mazirek) and Grave Pact turn every sacrifice into pressure. Announce each drain trigger and who loses life.
- Slow Grow: use the edict and removal loops on threats only, not to lock a player out, and no knockouts before turn 8. Full Send: loop Grave Pact with sac fodder every turn and close with Grave Titan, Kokusho or Living Death.
- Keep Bojuka Bog and Krosan Grip for opposing graveyard or artifact engines; keep a wipe (Damnation, Pernicious Deed) for when you are behind on board.
