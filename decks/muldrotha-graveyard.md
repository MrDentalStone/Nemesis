## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 to 5 lands plus a ramp spell or a self-mill creature (Stitcher's Supplier, Satyr Wayfinder, Hedron Crab). No-land or all-land hands go back.
- Turns 1 to 4: ramp and fill the graveyard. Land drops first, then mill pieces. Muldrotha comes down on turn 5 or 6; Greaves or Boots first if the table has removal open.
- Muldrotha rule: on each of your turns you may play one permanent card of each type from your graveyard (one creature, one land, one artifact, one enchantment, one planeswalker). Say which slot you are using ("Muldrotha, playing Evolving Wilds from the yard as my land") so the table can follow.
- Value loops: replay fetch lands and Myriad Landscape every turn for ramp; recast Fleshbag Marauder, Plaguecrafter, Shriekmaw or Ravenous Chupacabra as repeatable removal; Eternal Witness and Spore Frog over and over; Seal of Primordium and Executioner's Capsule are reusable removal too.
- Engines to protect: Ramunap Excavator or Crucible of Worlds (lands from yard every turn), Life from the Loam, Sylvan Library, Skullclamp on small creatures. Bojuka Bog can be replayed to keep exiling a rival graveyard.
- Win: a big late board (Grave Titan, Massacre Wurm, Avenger of Zendikar, Hydroid Krasis) or Living Death after a heavy self-mill. Lord of Extinction gets huge with both graveyards.
- Slow Grow: hold Living Death, Massacre Wurm and the wipes (Damnation, Pernicious Deed) until a player threatens to win; use the recursion for value and spot removal on whoever is ahead.
- Full Send: count lethal out loud before swinging; cast Living Death or Massacre Wurm when it swings the race, and keep Counterspell or Arcane Denial up for the opponents' wipe or combo.
