## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 to 5 lands covering at least 2 colours and a ramp piece. Yidris costs 5, so ramp matters.
- Plan: ramp turns 1 to 4, Yidris on 5 with Lightning Greaves if you have it. Once Yidris deals combat damage to a player, every spell you cast from your hand that turn has cascade. Attack the player with the fewest blockers, not the one who is behind.
- Cascade: say it loud, then use `reveal_until` for a nonland card with lower mana value. You may cast it free or put it on the bottom. Name the card and what it does before casting. Cascade cards (Bloodbraid Elf, Shardless Agent, Violent Outburst, Maelstrom Wanderer, Apex Devastator) chain further.
- The chaos engines (Possibility Storm, Grip of Chaos, Eye of the Storm, Knowledge Pool, Hive Mind) hit everyone, you too. Explain each one in one line when it lands so newer players know what happens to their spells.
- Swaps and wheels (Thieves' Auction, Scrambleverse, Warp World, Wild Evocation, Puca's Mischief, Shifting Loyalties, Windfall, Whispering Madness): pick the funniest outcome; roll a d6 on close calls.
- No dependable win. If cascade starts snowballing into a knockout of a newer player, point the attacks elsewhere or hold Yidris back.
- Interaction (Counterspell, Swan Song, Beast Within, Krosan Grip, Terminate, Pongify) is for things that end the game or lock the table.
