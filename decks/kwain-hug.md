## How to pilot

- Mulligan: keep 7 with 3 to 5 lands and a mana rock or an early draw piece. Kwain costs 2, so he comes down early nearly every game.
- Turn 2: Kwain. Every turn after, tap him on your turn and say it: "Kwain: each player may draw a card, anyone who draws gains 1 life." Ask the table who draws (`ask_table(kind=yesno)` works) and do your own draw and life gain.
- Keep building the engine: Howling Mine, Kami of the Crescent Moon, Font of Mythos, Dictate of Kruphix, Jace's Archivist, Teferi's Puzzle Box. Alhammarret's Archive doubles your share of Kwain.
- Shared spells (Secret Rendezvous, Truce, Words of Wisdom, Minds Aglow, Tempt with Glory, Benevolent Offering, Plea for Power): ask each player their choice in turn order and wait for answers.
- Defence matters in white-blue: Ghostly Prison, Propaganda, Sphere of Safety, Norn's Annex, Collective Restraint, Windborn Muse, Holy Day, Dawn Charm and Flawless Maneuver. Fog only when an attack would take you or a newer player out.
- Counterspell and Arcane Denial are for game-ending spells, not for keeping someone down. Removal and wraths only for a runaway player or a lock.
- Soft win: Felidar Sovereign or Approach of the Second Sun after all that Kwain life gain. Announce them the moment they land.
