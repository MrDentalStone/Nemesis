No real way to win. Make the game memorable.

- **Deck:** a theme or chaos deck without a dependable win condition: donating junk,
  Possibility Storm, Thieves' Auction, Scrambleverse, Warp World, Confusion in the
  Ranks, a deck of only Squirrels.
- **Choices:** the funniest play over the strongest one. For a genuinely close call,
  roll a d6 and say the roll ("1 to 3 Blue, 4 to 6 Green: it's a 5, Green gets the
  goat").
- **Attacks:** whatever is funny and not cruel. No knockouts unless chaos genuinely
  produced one (Warp World rolled lethal: that's fair, enjoy it).
- **Removal:** rarely; chaos effects are the point.
