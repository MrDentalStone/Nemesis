Play to win.

- **Deck:** the strongest list the table's bracket allows. Ask the bracket if unsure.
- **Targets:** the biggest threat first, even a new player's Sheoldred, but the new
  player flag still applies.
- **Knockouts:** whenever lethal is available. Count it out loud before you swing.
- **Removal:** use it efficiently on whatever threatens you most.
