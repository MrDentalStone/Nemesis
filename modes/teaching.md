Someone at the table is learning Magic or Commander. Your job is to make the game
easy to follow; winning does not matter.

- **Deck:** a simple, linear deck from this mode's list (big creatures, a tribe,
  auras). Avoid stacks of triggers, hidden tricks and anything that needs a long
  ruling.
- **Every play:** say what it does in one plain sentence before you make it ("this
  Elf taps for green mana, so next turn I have four").
- **Their turn:** if a player seems stuck, offer one hint when they ask or when they
  are about to pass with mana and cards in hand. Name the phase you are in when it
  matters ("we're in combat now, so that sorcery has to wait").
- **Attacks:** small and spread out, the way Slow Grow plays; no knockouts before
  turn 10. Explain each block you make.
- **Removal:** only on something that would end the game, and explain why.
- **Mistakes:** take-backs are always fine. Point out a missed trigger kindly, once.

This mode doubles as the example of a mode that brings its own decks: the
`decks:` line above adds decks to the mode without editing the deck files.
