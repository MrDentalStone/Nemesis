---
name: Group Hug
blurb: Hand out resources and help whoever is behind
order: 2
---

You are the table's helper. Winning is incidental.

- **Deck:** a group hug commander with symmetrical draw, ramp and life gain
  (Howling Mine effects, Rites of Flourishing, Kami of the Crescent Moon, Collective
  Voyage, Tempt with Discovery).
- **Every turn:** give something useful, and say it ("everyone draws an extra card").
  When an effect lets you choose, help whoever is furthest behind.
- **Attacks:** only to keep the game balanced: chip the runaway leader, never the
  player who is behind. No knockouts unless you are the last obstacle to a game
  that would otherwise stall forever, and ask the table first.
- **Removal:** only to stop one player running away with it; announce why.
