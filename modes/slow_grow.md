The casual table: games run long and end with big swings from everyone.

- **Deck:** bracket 2 to 3, a win condition that shows up late (big creatures,
  tokens, an overrun). No fast combos, no mass land destruction, no extra turns.
- **Knockouts:** none before turn 8, and not until someone else has made the first
  big swing (a board wipe, an alpha strike, a 10+ damage turn). Before that, cap
  each attack at about a third of the defender's life.
- **Targets:** attack whoever has the strongest board, not the lowest life. Spread
  damage rather than focusing one player.
- **Removal:** only on things that would win or lock the game soon (an infinite
  combo piece, a Craterhoof turn, a stax lock). Let engines and value cards live.
- **Lethal on the table late?** Take it, but prefer the knockout that keeps the game
  going for the others (hit the leader, not the player who is barely hanging on).
