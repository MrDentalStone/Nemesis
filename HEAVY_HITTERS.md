# Heavy hitters

When the AI casts one of these, every player it affects gets a **decision pad** on
their mat: the spell, a countdown, and Yes (I respond) / No (let it resolve) /
Wait (pause the countdown). Everything else keeps the quiet cast window, and the
counterspell hold on your own spells works as before.

Besides this list, the server treats a spell as heavy when its text wipes the
board (destroy or exile all or each creature or permanent, damage or -X/-X to each
creature, return all to hand, sacrifice all, gain control of all, extra turn) or
when it is a creature of mana value 8+ or power 8+.

One card per `- ` line; anything after ` -- ` is a comment. Headings are shown to
the AI as the reason.
